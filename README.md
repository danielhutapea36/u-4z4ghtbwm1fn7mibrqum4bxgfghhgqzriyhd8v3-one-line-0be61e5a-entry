## The line

> My phone autocorrected 'I'm fine' to 'I'm broke' — honestly, it's the first time technology has truly understood me.

The joke is in the turn: the setup sounds like a complaint about a typo, then the punchline reframes it — the "error" is not an error at all, it is the most accurate thing the phone has ever said. Everyday, economical, and no AI or phone cliché.
